This game is called "Tavern Portfolio Advisor" or "The Iron Bank of Eldoria Portfolio Rebalancer".

The Tavern Portfolio Advisor is a fantasy wealth management simulator where players balance their vault treasury across three distinct asset classes over four volatile market cycles (representing different seasons and events). 

OBJECTIVE
Optimize your total wealth starting from 10,000 Gold while a Bayesian Master observes your decisions to determine your investor risk profile: Conservative, Moderate, or Risky.

ASSET CLASSES
1. Dwarven Gold Bonds & Vault CDs (Conservative Asset)
   - Profile: Low Risk
   - Behavior: Steady, guaranteed growth with minimal fluctuations.
   - Return rates vary from +1% to +3% per turn depending on cycles.
   
2. Merchant Guild Real Estate & Trading Stocks (Moderate Asset)
   - Profile: Medium Risk
   - Behavior: Stable long-term growth with moderate ups and downs.
   - Return rates vary from -5% to +12% per turn.

3. Mana Crystal Crypto & Gnomish Startups (Risky Asset)
   - Profile: High Risk
   - Behavior: Extreme volatility, high risk, high return.
   - Return rates vary from -40% to +80% per turn.

MARKET SEASONS / CYCLES
- Cycle 1: Stable Harvest (Stable Market)
  - Dwarven Bonds & CDs: +0.8%
  - Guild Real Estate & Stocks: +2.5%
  - Mana Crypto & Startups: +5.0%
- Cycle 2: Orc Invasion (Bear Market Crash)
  - Dwarven Bonds & CDs: -0.2%
  - Guild Real Estate & Stocks: -6.0%
  - Mana Crypto & Startups: -24.0%
- Cycle 3: Elven Golden Age (Bull Market Rally)
  - Dwarven Bonds & CDs: +1.2%
  - Guild Real Estate & Stocks: +8.5%
  - Mana Crypto & Startups: +32.0%
- Cycle 4: Wizard's Tempest (Highly Volatile)
  - Dwarven Bonds & CDs: +0.5%
  - Guild Real Estate & Stocks: -1.5%
  - Mana Crypto & Startups: -15.0%

REBALANCING STRATEGY PRESETS
- Conservative Strategy:
  - 70% Bonds & CDs, 20% Real Estate & Stocks, 10% Crypto & Startups
- Moderate Strategy:
  - 20% Bonds & CDs, 60% Real Estate & Stocks, 20% Crypto & Startups
- Risky Strategy:
  - 10% Bonds & CDs, 15% Real Estate & Stocks, 75% Crypto & Startups

BAYESIAN RISK PROFILER
Each turn, the advisor updates the player's profile probabilities based on their choice.
The prior beliefs start at 33.3% each (Conservative, Moderate, Risky).
The posterior probabilities are calculated using Bayes' Theorem:
P(Profile | Choice) = P(Choice | Profile) * P(Profile) / P(Choice)

Where P(Choice | Profile) is the Likelihood of choosing a particular rebalance preset given the investor's true risk profile.
For example, a Conservative investor is highly likely (80%) to select the Conservative Strategy during a Bear Market, while a Risky investor is highly likely (70%) to choose the Risky Strategy to "buy the dip".
Based on the leading probability, the Bayesian Master gives tailored strategic wealth advice to secure your gold treasury.
